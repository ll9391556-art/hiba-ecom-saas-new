🚀 About Our Platform

What is OrderFlow AI?

OrderFlow AI is a complete SaaS (Software as a Service) platform designed specifically for e-commerce businesses in Algeria and the Arab world. We automate the entire sales management workflow, from order intake to delivery tracking.

📦

Order Management — Track all orders with real-time status updates (Pending, Confirmed, Delivered, Refused)

🤖

AI Bot (WhatsApp & Facebook) — Gemini-powered chatbot that automatically responds to customers and creates orders from conversations

🛒

Online Store — Each merchant gets a public catalog page with cart functionality for customers

📊

Dashboard & Analytics — Real-time profits, confirmation rates, top products, and low stock alerts

🏙️

City & Shipping Management — Configure shipping costs per city with automatic profit calculation

Our platform serves Algerian e-commerce sellers who use Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp to sell products. We help them organize orders, automate responses, and grow their business.

🛡️ Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When merchants connect their Facebook or Instagram pages to OrderFlow AI, we collect:

  • Facebook Page ID and access tokens (to receive and send messages)
  • Instagram Business Account ID and access tokens
  • Customer messages sent to connected pages (for AI bot processing only)
  • Order information provided by customers during conversations (name, phone, city, product)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Order Processing: Customer messages are processed by our AI to extract order details and create orders in the merchant's dashboard
  • Automated Responses: Our AI bot responds to customers on behalf of merchants
  • Analytics: We calculate profits, confirmation rates, and stock levels for merchants
  • We do NOT sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties

3. Data Storage

  • All data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Europe West region)
  • Facebook access tokens are stored encrypted and used solely to communicate with Meta APIs
  • Chat histories are stored per-user and accessible only to the merchant
  • Merchants can delete their data at any time by contacting us

4. Meta Platform Data

  • We access Facebook and Instagram APIs only with explicit merchant permission via OAuth
  • We only request permissions necessary for order automation: pages_messaging, instagram_manage_messages, pages_read_engagement
  • We do not access personal profiles, friend lists, or any data beyond page messages
  • Merchants can disconnect their Facebook/Instagram at any time from the Settings page

5. Data Retention

We retain merchant data for as long as the account is active. Upon account deletion request, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, Firebase security rules, and token-based authentication to protect your data.
